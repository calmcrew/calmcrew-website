View history

Your mood and energy, charted.

Check-ins add the mood and energy you rate to your own record. View history charts it, and check-ins made straight after a practice get a line of their own. If the lines part ways, that’s a pattern, not proof of a cause.

At sea

Works offline, on board.

  • No signal needed

    Check-ins save on your phone. iCloud sync needs internet.

  • Sync to iCloud

    Optional.

  • Day and Night

    One light, one dark.

  • Reminders

    Opt-in. Your hours.

The app

Three tabs. That’s the lot.

Along the bottom: Today, Learn, S.O.S.

  • Today

    “How are you now?” Sea state: Calm, Steady, Choppy, Rough or Storm. Energy: Low to High. Two taps.

  • Learn

    The four-chapter course.

  • S.O.S.

    A separate tab for the hard moments. More below.

S.O.S.

For the hard moments.

The third tab is a quiet corner.

A few short practices for settling yourself, then routes to a person: someone on board, the bridge, a seafarers’ helpline (details being confirmed before the pilot).

Calm Crew is not a crisis or emergency service. In an emergency at sea, go to the bridge. Ashore, call the local emergency number.

The practices aren’t recorded yet.

The course

Four chapters.

By Libby Pickett.

Centred on The Core Journey.

  • Chapter 1

    Understand your nervous system

    The three-state model: how your nervous system moves between states and senses safety and danger.

  • Chapter 2

    The Core Journey

    Pre-boarding, then seven modules:

    1. Welcome aboard
    2. Meet your vagus nerve
    3. Breathing below deck
    4. Sleep in a world that never stops
    5. Calm under pressure
    6. Somatic tools for small spaces
    7. Your long game

  • Chapter 3

    The Leadership Track

    For captains and heads of department. How it opens is undecided.

  • Chapter 4

    Practice library

    Planned: short practices. Opens once you finish The Core Journey.

Privacy

Anonymous. Now, and later.
By design.

  • Not shared

    Your name and identifiable data.

  • Your data. Your iCloud sync.

    You own your data. Always.

  • What management sees

    Only whole-crew energy and mood, aggregated: a feel for how everyone’s doing that fills in as the crew uses the app. And only with your extra Bluetooth authorisation.

The pilot

A study, not a claim.

The pilot: iPhone only.

Who the app is for

  • Crew

    Hear first: join the waiting list.

  • Captains and heads of department

  • Vessels and management

    Wanted: a handful of vessels to contribute crew to November’s pilot. Founding partners: named founding status, free programme for participating crew, first look at the overall findings. No vessel or company sees anyone’s own answers.

  • Boat industry leaders

    Bring Calm Crew to your crews. Any energy and mood view: crew-wide, never individual, opt-in per person.
