View history

Your mood and energy, over time.

Every check-in adds to your own record of the mood and energy you rate. Tap View history on Today to see it as bars by day, week, month or year, with your averages. Your streak sits on the same screen, and Learn shows where you are in the course. Look across the three: anything that seems to move together is a pattern, not proof of a cause.

The course

Four chapters.

Teachings, practices and short guides by Libby Pickett.

At its centre is The Core Journey. In the pilot, the programme runs for eight weeks.

  • Chapter 1

    Understand your nervous system

    The three states your nervous system moves between, how it senses safety and danger (neuroception), and small cues of safety (glimmers). Two teachings to begin with.

  • Chapter 2

    The Core Journey

    Pre-boarding, then seven modules, each a teaching with its practice:

    1. Welcome aboard
    2. Meet your vagus nerve
    3. Breathing below deck
    4. Sleep in a world that never stops
    5. Calm under pressure
    6. Somatic tools for small spaces
    7. Your long game

  • Chapter 3

    The Leadership Track

    For heads of department and captains. Six lessons, each a teaching and a practice. How it opens is still being decided.

  • Chapter 4

    Practice library

    Short practices, planned in ten families, from orienting and breath to stillness. Opens when you finish The Core Journey.

The app

Three tabs. That is the whole app.

Today, Learn and Drop Anchor sit along the bottom of the screen. Settings at your command with the three dots in the corner.

  • Today

    One question: “How are you now?” Tap a sea state — Calm, Steady, Choppy, Rough or Storm — and an energy level from Low to High. Two taps, and it is saved. Check in as often as you like.

  • Learn

    The course, in four chapters. Each module of The Core Journey pairs a teaching with its practice. The recordings are still to be made.

  • Drop Anchor

    A separate tab for the hard moments. More about it below.

Drop Anchor

For the hard moments.

The third tab is a quiet corner of the app. No streak, no settings, no alarm colours, nothing moving.

A few short practices for settling yourself, then routes to a person: someone on board, the bridge, and a seafarers’ helpline whose details are being confirmed before the pilot. There is a short page, too, for a crewmate helping someone else.

Calm Crew is not a crisis or emergency service. In an emergency at sea, go to the bridge. Ashore, call the local emergency number.

The practices are still to be recorded.

Privacy

What management sees.

Anonymous. Now, and later.
By design.

  • Anonymous

    Your name and identifiable data is not shared.

  • Your Data. Your iCloud sync.

    You own your data. Always.

  • What management sees.

    Aggregated data from the whole crew to have a feel for how’s everyone doing in Energy levels + Mood, the more the crew utilizes the Calm Crew app. This requires an additional Bluetooth connection authorization by the user.

The pilot

A study, not a claim.

We are not going to tell you what this does until we have measured it.

From November 2026, around forty crew across six to ten vessels run the full eight-week programme as a formal pilot study: validated measures at four points, an independent clinical advisor, and results that will say what they say.

For the pilot, the app is iPhone only.

Who it’s for

  • Crew

    For the deckhand, stew, engineer or chief who intends to be good at this for a long time. Join the waiting list and you’ll hear first.

  • Captains and heads of department

    You carry the vessel’s load and your own, and you are the one everybody else reads for weather. The Leadership Track is being made for you. How it opens is still being decided. Join the waiting list to hear first.

  • Vessels and management

    We are looking for a handful of vessels to contribute crew to the November pilot. Founding partners get named founding status, the programme at no cost for their participating crew, and a first look at the study’s overall findings. No vessel or company sees any crew member’s own answers.

  • Industry advisors

    One or two senior voices — a management lead, an agency principal, a captain of standing — helping shape what this becomes.

About Libby

Why this, and why me.

Calm Crew comes out of five years of one-to-one practice, group work and retreats, with yacht crew and industry people among them.

I’m Libby Pickett. I teach nervous-system regulation, and I live in Palma de Mallorca.

What I kept hearing was not people asking for help. It was people asking how to keep going at this standard — how to still be sharp in month seven, and still be themselves at the end of a season.
